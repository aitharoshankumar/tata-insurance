Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026 • Independent advisor enquiry website

This website is operated for insurance information and enquiries associated with Aitha Roshan Kumar. It is not the official corporate website of Tata AIA Life Insurance Company Limited.

Information collected

If you choose to submit an enquiry, the website collects your name, mobile number, email address, planning interest, preferred contact method and affirmative contact consent. Do not share financial account numbers, identity documents or medical details via this form.

Purpose and contact

Your enquiry details are used to respond to your request and discuss potential insurance options. Your form submission is transmitted to the configured enquiry inbox only after email delivery is successfully configured. We do not publish your details or sell them.

Storage and third-party services

Enquiries may be retained in the receiving email system and by its email provider. Site hosting, email delivery and operational logging providers may process technical data. Only a properly configured server should be used to accept enquiries; do not email sensitive documents through this website.

Your choices

To ask for access, correction, deletion or to withdraw future contact consent, email roshantatainsurance@gmail.com. Requests will be handled subject to applicable legal obligations. Withdrawal will not affect a message already sent in response to an earlier request.

External links and images

Some product links open the official Tata AIA website. Photos are loaded from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts; those providers may receive technical request data. This website does not intentionally deploy advertising trackers.

Before launch

The website operator must confirm the business identity, applicable privacy obligations, data retention procedures and the production hosting/email provider practices before collecting real customer enquiries.
