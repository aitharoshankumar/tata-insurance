01 / LIFE PROTECTION

Protection for the people you love.

Life insurance can help your nominees manage financial commitments after a covered event, subject to the chosen policy's terms.

  • Explore cover based on responsibilities and dependants
  • Understand premiums, exclusions and claim conditions
  • Discuss available term insurance options

04 / WEALTH & SAVINGS

Make thoughtful moves for tomorrow.

Compare available insurance savings products. Some plans offer benefits defined in the contract, while market-linked plans expose investment values to market risk.

  • Understand policy-specific guaranteed benefits, if any
  • Distinguish non-linked and market-linked plans
  • Review charges, lock-ins and surrender conditions
“Small steps today can build a stronger financial tomorrow.”

Look forward to the years ahead.

  • Understand available payout structures

06 / ADDITIONAL PROTECTION

Explore protection beyond the basics.

Optional riders may provide additional coverage with certain policies. Availability and benefits depend on product compatibility, underwriting and terms.

  • Review suitable optional rider categories
  • Check eligibility and extra premium, if applicable
  • Understand definitions, exclusions and limits
